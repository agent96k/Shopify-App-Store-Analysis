# Shopify-App-Store-Analysis
Power BI analysis of Shopify App Store data - app ratings, categories, and review trends.

Reporting Period: 2018-2024

<sub>some numbers are shortened for legibility, all insights are pulled from the data provided. completed October 24, 2026 by Kassandra White.</sub>

**Key Insight:** The most popular category is Sales and Conversion, with 49 apps, 916 reviews, and an average rating of 4.35. Overall, Shopify hosts 500 apps with 8,000 total reviews and an average rating of 4.19. Review volume has grown dramatically over time — from just 2 reviews between July–November 2018 to 8,000 total today, with half of all reviews occurring in 2024 alone.

**Business Impact:** This shows that users rely on Shopify most for Sales and Conversion apps, meaning it's likely a key driver of merchant success and platform loyalty.

**Recommendation:** Shopify should continue prioritizing Sales and Conversion apps — this category leads in app count, review volume, average rating, and developer reply rate, making it the platform's strongest-performing segment.
